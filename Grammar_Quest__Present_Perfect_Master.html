Structure: Subject + HAVE / HAS + Past Participle

Use has for He/She/It; have for I/You/We/They.

For vs. Since:

FOR: Duration of time (e.g., 3 hours, 5 years, a long time).

SINCE: Specific starting point (e.g., 2010, Monday, 8:00 AM).

Ever & Never:

EVER: In questions ("Have you ever visited London?").

NEVER: Negative statements ("I have never seen snow").

Already & Yet:

ALREADY: Sooner than expected (mid-position: "has already finished").

YET: Expected event, end of questions/negatives ("not yet").
